English

This Privacy Policy explains how NY Kebaya ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information through our WhatsApp auto-reply system.

Information We Collect

When you contact us via WhatsApp, we collect:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • The display name set on your WhatsApp profile
  • The content of the messages you send us

How We Use This Information

We use this information only to:

  • Respond to your inquiries (e.g. pricing, bookings, business hours)
  • Keep track of whether you've already received our introduction, to avoid sending it twice
  • Schedule and confirm your bookings

We do not sell your information or share it with unrelated third parties.

Data Storage

Your contact information is stored in the internal data tables of our automation system (n8n), used solely for this business's own customer service.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, or would like your stored information deleted, please contact us via WhatsApp at +60169355078.
